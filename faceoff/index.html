How to play
  • Pick your character. The bot secretly picks one too.
  • On your turn, either ask a yes/no question or guess who the bot has.
  • After an answer, tap cards to flip down anyone it rules out, then end your turn.
  • Wrong guess and you lose on the spot, so be sure.
  • The bot answers honestly and asks about your character on its turn. The counts up top show how many faces each of you has left.
  • Easy bot asks random questions. Hard bot always picks the question that cuts its list closest to half.
