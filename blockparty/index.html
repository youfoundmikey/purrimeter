How to play
  • Drag the three pieces onto the board. You get three new ones once all three are down.
  • Fill a whole row or column and it clears. Clear several at once for big points.
  • Clear on back-to-back drops to build a combo streak.
  • Pieces don't rotate. When none of them fit anywhere, it's game over.
  • Undos are earned: every 3 multi-line clears banks you 1 more.
  • Hard mode deals bigger, uglier pieces and never checks that one fits.
