How to play
  • Every row, every column, and every color zone gets exactly one cat.
  • Cats can't touch. Not side to side, not even corner to corner.
  • Every puzzle has one solution, so it's all logic. Guess wrong and you lose a heart. Three strikes and you're out.
  • Mark spots that can't be cats with ✕. On desktop, right-click drops a cat.
  • Hints are earned: every 3 boards you clear without a mistake banks you 1 hint.
Daily puzzle resets at midnight, your time.
