How to play
  • Start on the black dot and drag one continuous line through every block.
  • Only up, down, left and right. You can't cross your own line or visit a block twice.
  • Tap anywhere on your line to cut it back to that block.
  • Holes in the board are off limits. The shape is the whole challenge.
  • A hint draws the next 3 correct moves, and rewinds you first if you've gone off course.
  • Hints are earned: every 3 boards you clear without a hint banks you 1 more.
Daily puzzle resets at midnight, your time.
